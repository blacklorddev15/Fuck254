Frequently Asked Questions
How do I pair my WhatsApp bot?
Go to WhatsApp Pairing, select your bot, enter your phone number with country code, and click Generate Pairing Code.
Can I link multiple phone numbers?
Each phone number is restricted to one active session at a time to prevent duplicate queues.
How do I access my Pterodactyl panel?
Visit the Server Management section in your dashboard to view your container credentials and direct panel link.
